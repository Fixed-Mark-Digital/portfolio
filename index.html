Accessibility · Retail

NYPL Shop

WCAG 2.2 AA remediation of a Shopify store

The problem

A third-party accessibility audit flagged 51 distinct issues across the site, including 8 critical ones that blocked keyboard and screen reader users from navigation, search, cart and checkout.

The outcome

All 51 issues confirmed fixed through iterative retesting, with zero violations outstanding at handoff.

What I did

Page-by-page remediation across the full Shopify theme: accessible navigation and modals, corrected ARIA roles and focus management, color contrast fixes, semantic headings, labeled form fields, and keyboard-accessible carousels and date pickers. Every fix went through a retest cycle with the auditing firm.

Shopify · POS

Cold Diggers Ice Cream

Online store and in-person point of sale

The problem

A brick-and-mortar ice cream shop tracked online and in-store inventory separately. Keeping the two in sync by hand caused errors and took time every week.

What I did

I built a Shopify storefront for online pre-orders and set up Shopify POS for in-person sales. Both channels run from one product catalog, so there is nothing to reconcile.

The outcome

Online and in-store sales now draw from the same inventory, with stock levels, sales and reporting in one place.
